CONCLUSION
Channel C is NOT an exact zero. For the historical preparation,
|beta_H|^2 = 2.47418e-5, stable under endpoint-cutoff refinement.
The old tabulated 0.00002 is a rounded finite-window estimate.
The small value is not preparation-invariant: changing r0 or omitting
the WKB amplitude-derivative term changes |beta_H|^2 by factors of
several to ~10^2. Exact zero-squeeze points do exist as isolated simple
zeros of the matching map, but their positions move with r0, demonstrating
that they are preparation-dependent resonances, not universal channels.
